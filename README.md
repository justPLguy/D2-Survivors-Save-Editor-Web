## Why the D2 Survivors editor lives in the browser now

The older downloadable builds of the **D2 Survivors editor** kept running into the same two problems: people had to **download and install** something just to tweak a save, and antivirus programs kept throwing **false-positive virus warnings** at a tool that was never doing anything harmful — scaring players off before they even tried it.

So we did what players were actually asking for: **we moved the whole thing into the browser.** No installer, nothing for your antivirus to panic about — you just open a link and the **D2 Survivors save editor** runs. And because it is a web page, it works the same on **PC and on your phone**, for free, with your save staying on your own device. This repo was an early step on that road; the link above is where it continues.
